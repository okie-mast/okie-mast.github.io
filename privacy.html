Privacy Policy

Personal Rclone Backup is a private backup setup for my own use. It is not offered as a service to anyone else.

Google Drive access

I authorize rclone to access my Google Drive account so it can copy selected files from my computers to Drive and check the backups. Rclone uses the permissions I grant through Google's consent screen.

Storage and sharing

Backup files are stored in my Google Drive account. This public website describes the setup; it does not receive or store backup files or Google authorization credentials.

Removing access

I can revoke rclone's access in my Google Account settings and delete backup files directly in Google Drive.
